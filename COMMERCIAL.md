# Commercial permission and services

The source-available terms are [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE.txt).
They expressly permit the listed government and nonprofit organization uses;
these grants must not be presented as requiring a commercial license fee.
Other commercial purposes require separate permission from the authorized
rights holder. The published terms, rather than the customer's industry alone,
determine whether additional permission is needed.

To discuss permission, contact support@archymedes.xyz with the subject
"ARGUS commercial permission". Describe the intended use, organization type,
version or commit, deployment/hosting model, redistribution and support needs.
Do not send credentials, private logs, documents or security evidence.
An inquiry, invoice, payment or activation key is not itself a signed agreement.

Before accepting payment for commercial permission, maintainers must verify the
actual contracting entity, its authority over all covered code, the version and
scope, permitted use/redistribution/hosting, term and support responsibilities.
No price, SLA, indemnity, warranty, certification or service availability is
promised by this preparation. Existing customers retain their actual contracts.

Potential paid offerings include authorized commercial deployment permissions,
managed operations, integrations, maintained releases, analyst review and
support. Government institutions with a permitted source use may still buy
services voluntarily. Core collection, detection, evidence, recovery and alerts
remain independent of payment and entitlement state. No runtime paywall is added.

Earlier AGPL recipients keep their rights, including commercial use of those
versions under AGPL. This new offering cannot eliminate competition from those
versions. Public release remains pending privacy/disclosure review and required
validation. Before a sale, also verify the actual contracting authority and the
[commercial-rights record](docs/commercial/CONTRIBUTOR-RIGHTS.md).
