# Contributor and commercial licensing rights

Status: required record; no executed CLA, assignment or commercial grant is
asserted by this document. Do not store private contracts in the public tree.

The owner confirmed in the 2026-11-17 session that both original coauthors have
authority to release their work under AGPL and that no other rights holder needs
to consent. The owner then selected noncommercial source availability plus paid
commercial permission. In a separate explicit reply the owner confirmed that
both authors authorize this revised arrangement for their contributions. This is
an owner-reported rights record; no copyright assignment, executed CLA or
incorporation is asserted. It does not replace grants for future outside work.

Before publication or a commercial sale, a maintainer records:

- The covered authors/rights holders, exact code or commit scope, and any
  employment, contractor, imported-code or data-rights exclusions.
- Authority for the new PolyForm Noncommercial distribution and the proposed
  separate commercial permissions; all necessary rights holders must grant it.
- A reference to the executed agreement or explicit rights-holder record in
  private records, its date, reviewer and permitted scope. Publish only a
  redacted status record; never publish signatures or private contract details.
- An inventory showing that third-party terms and earlier AGPL grants remain
  respected. A new own-code license does not relicense dependencies or old grants.

For future contributions, DCO certifies provenance under the submitted license;
it does not grant the additional commercial rights needed for this model.
Maintainers may discuss proposals and synthetic reproductions, but must not merge
externally copyrighted contributions until an approved contributor
agreement or other documented grant supports both distribution paths, including documentation, rules and assets that
will be part of the commercial offering. An existing permissive license may
supply the necessary grant when reviewed and its notices are retained. A draft
CLA is not an executed grant, and no contributor is asked to sign an unreviewed
agreement. Copyright assignment is not assumed or required by this document.
