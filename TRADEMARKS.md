# Project names and release identity

ARGUS-1, ARGUS-INSTINCT, Archymedes and project logos identify the official
Archymedes project and its releases. This policy asserts no registration and
makes no change to the software license. It does not restrict truthful
attribution, comparisons, nominative references or other lawful uses.

You may describe a fork as “based on ARGUS-1” and retain required copyright,
license and historical attribution notices. Clearly label modified distributions
as unofficial and identify their maintainer. Use a distinct product name and
visual identity for your fork's product, download site and releases so users
can tell who supplies and supports them. Do not imply Archymedes endorsement,
reuse official signing keys, or present your artifacts as official releases.

The official source home is https://github.com/Archymedes-Lab/argus-1.
Check release provenance using the documented release-signing process and an
independently obtained signer fingerprint. A copied logo is not verification.
Requests for uses implying partnership or endorsement go to the maintainers.
