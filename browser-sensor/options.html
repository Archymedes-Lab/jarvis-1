JARVIS Veil

Reporting to JARVIS

Sends aggregate metadata (counters and origins) to JARVIS on this machine only. Never page text, typed characters, pointer positions, cookies, paths or query strings.

Blocking (off by default)

Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, FullStory, LogRocket, Mouseflow, Smartlook, Contentsquare, Yandex Webvisor and similar. They record your mouse movement, clicks, scrolling and, where a site forgets to mask it, what you type. Blocking them can remove a site's feedback survey; it never stops a page loading.

Known ad and analytics pixel endpoints (Meta, Google Analytics, Bing, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, X, Snapchat) and mail-open pixels. Conversion tracking stops working; pages and links do not.

The toolbar badge shows how many requests were blocked on the current tab. Untick a box to undo it at once.
